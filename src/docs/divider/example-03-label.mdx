---
title: Divider with a label
description: A label below the line, such as a section heading, isn't a Divider property but a layout around it. M3 puts 4px between the line and the label (`mt-1` on the label) and 8px at the end and bottom (`pe-2 pb-2` on the block). M3 doesn't name a type style for the label; here it's Title Small in `on-surface-variant`. Pick the heading level that fits the page's outline.
