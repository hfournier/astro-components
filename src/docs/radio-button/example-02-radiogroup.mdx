---
title: RadioButton group without a fieldset
description: Without a `<fieldset>`, give the group `role="radiogroup"` and name it with `aria-labelledby`. An option such as "None" lets people take back their choice, since a selected group can't be cleared.
---

<div role="radiogroup" aria-labelledby="radio-button-example-notifications" class="flex flex-col items-start">
  <span id="radio-button-example-notifications">Notifications</span>
  <RadioButton name="example-notifications" value="all" checked>All</RadioButton>
  <RadioButton name="example-notifications" value="mentions">Mentions only</RadioButton>
  <RadioButton name="example-notifications" value="none">None</RadioButton>
</div>
