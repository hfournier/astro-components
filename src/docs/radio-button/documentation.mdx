## Attributes

Every attribute except `class` goes on the `<input>`: `name`, `value`, `checked`, `disabled`, `required`, `form`, `aria-*` and the rest. `class` goes on the `<label>` around it, so it can place and space the whole control. Listen for the input's `change` event to react to a change.

## Groups

Give every radio button in a group the same `name`. Choosing one clears the others, and the form submits the chosen one's `value`. Put the group in a `<fieldset>` with a `<legend>` that names it, or in an element with `role="radiogroup"` and `aria-labelledby`. M3 stacks the options vertically: give the group `flex flex-col items-start`.

## States

- **Unselected:** a 2px ring in `on-surface-variant` (the background's variant on-color inside a BaseWrapper). Hovered, focused or pressed, it darkens to the label's color.
- **Selected** (`checked`): a `primary` ring with a 10px `primary` dot.
- **Disabled** (`disabled`): the ring and dot in `on-surface` at 38%, and the label at 38%.

The state layer is a 40px circle, at 8% when hovered and 10% when focused or pressed. It's the label's color (`on-surface` outside any wrapper) when unselected and `primary` when selected. While pressed it takes the other color, as M3's tokens give it.

## Motion

As in Compose, the dot grows from the center and shrinks back on the fast spatial spring, so it briefly overshoots its size, and the ring and dot change color on default effects. Compose puts the colors on default effects rather than the fast effects M3 uses for most small components, and RadioButton follows it (ADR-0018). Under reduced motion, every state changes at once.
