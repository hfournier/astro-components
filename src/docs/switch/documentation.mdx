## Attributes

Every attribute except `class` goes on the `<input>`: `name`, `value`, `checked`, `disabled`, `required`, `form`, `aria-*` and the rest. `class` goes on the `<label>` around it, so it can place and space the whole control. Listen for the input's `change` event to react to a change, and apply it at once: M3's switches take effect without a save step. In a form, a switch that's on submits its `value` (`on` by default), and one that's off submits nothing.

## States

- **Unselected:** a `surface-container-highest` track in a 2px `outline`, with a 16px `outline` handle at its start. Hovered, focused or pressed, the handle darkens to `on-surface-variant`.
- **Selected** (`checked`): a `primary` track with a 24px `on-primary` handle at its end. Hovered, focused or pressed, the handle turns `primary-container`.
- **Pressed:** the handle grows to 28px in place.
- **Disabled** (`disabled`): the track at 12% (`on-surface` when selected), the handle in `on-surface` at 38% when unselected and `surface` when selected, and the label at 38%.

With `icons`, the check is `primary` and the x `surface-container-highest`, as on M3's Specs page. Hovered, focused or pressed, the check turns `on-primary-container`, the on-color of the `primary-container` handle. Here Switch departs from M3, which keeps it `primary`: in a scheme whose `primary-container` is close to `primary` in tone, such as this theme's Fidelity, a `primary` check would all but vanish.

The state layer is a 40px circle centered on the handle, at 8% when hovered and 10% when focused or pressed. It's the label's color (`on-surface` outside any wrapper) when unselected and `primary` when selected.

## Motion

The handle slides and resizes on the fast spatial spring, so it briefly overshoots, and the colors and icons change on fast effects (ADR-0018). Pressed, the handle snaps to its 28px, as Compose's does, and springs from there to its new place on release. Under reduced motion, every state changes at once.
