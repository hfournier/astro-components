Use a slider to:

- pick a value from a range, such as volume, brightness or the strength of an image filter
- adjust a setting whose effect people can see or hear as they move it
- pick a minimum and a maximum, such as a price range

Changes take effect at once, as the handle moves. The track should show the whole range of values that can be chosen. Pick the variant from where the range starts:

- **Standard,** when it starts from zero or the beginning of a sequence.
- **Centered,** when zero, or the default value, is in the middle, with negative and positive values either side, such as an exposure adjustment.
- **Range,** for a minimum and a maximum, picked with two handles.

A slider can be horizontal or vertical; pick what suits the layout. Keep range sliders horizontal: two handles on a vertical track ask more of people, who are used to horizontal sliders. Larger sizes give a slider more emphasis, with `xl` kept for a slider that's the focus of its page.

### When not to use it

- **An exact value matters,** such as a quantity or a price: use a text field, or put one beside the slider, kept in step with it, that people can tab to right after it.
- **The change waits for a save or submit:** a slider's effect should show as it moves.
- **The range has a few named options,** such as small, medium and large: use radio buttons or a connected button group.
- **Turning a setting on or off:** use a switch.
