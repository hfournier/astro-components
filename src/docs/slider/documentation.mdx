## Attributes

Every attribute except `class` goes on the `<input>`: `name`, `min`, `max`, `step`, `value`, `disabled`, `form`, `id`, `aria-*` and the rest. `class` goes on the element around it, which draws the slider, so it can size and place it. A horizontal slider spans the width of its container; inside a flex row, give it a width.

The range is the input's: `min` 0, `max` 100 and `step` 1 unless set, and `value` halfway unless set. `max` must be more than `min` and `step` more than 0 (or `"any"`), or the build fails. Listen for the input's `input` event to follow the value as it moves, and `change` for the value once it's set: when the pointer is released, or for each key press. Apply a change at once: M3's sliders take effect as they move, so people see what their choice does.

To set the value from a script, set the input's `value` and dispatch an `input` event on it, so the slider redraws. A form's reset redraws it on its own.

A centered slider's middle is halfway between `min` and `max`, such as 0 from -50 to 50. Its value starts there unless set.

A range slider's two inputs share its `min`, `max`, `step`, `disabled` and `form`, and take their own `value`, `name`, `id` and `aria-*` from `handleStart` and `handleEnd`. Given at the top, those would go on both inputs, so they fail the build, as do shared attributes given to one handle. The handles can't cross: each stops at the other, whether it's moved by the pointer, the keys or a script. A press moves the nearer handle there, or with both at one value, the one on the side the pointer moves to.

```astro
<Slider
  variant="range"
  min="0"
  max="500"
  step="10"
  handleStart={{ name: "min-price", value: "100", "aria-label": "Minimum price" }}
  handleEnd={{ name: "max-price", value: "400", "aria-label": "Maximum price" }}
/>
```

## Anatomy

- **Active track:** `primary`. A standard slider's runs from the start of the track to 6px before the handle; a centered one's from the middle to 6px before the handle; a range one's from 6px after the start handle to 6px before the end one. Its outer corners are M3's size (8px for `xs` and `sm`, 12px `md`, 16px `lg`, 28px `xl`) and the ones beside a handle or the middle 2px. Shorter than its corner, it isn't drawn, as Compose's isn't.
- **Handle:** a `primary` line, 4px wide, across the track at the value. Pressed or focused from the keyboard, it narrows to 2px.
- **Inactive track:** `secondary-container`, from 6px after the handle to the end, with its corners the other way round. A centered or range slider has one at the start too, up to 6px before its handle or the middle.
- **Stop indicator:** a 4px circle, 4px in from the end of the inactive track (from each end on a centered or range slider), in `on-secondary-container`. M3's Specs page gives it this color; Compose draws it `primary`.
- **Stops:** with `showStops`, 4px circles evenly from 4px in from the start to the stop indicator: `on-primary` on the active track and `on-secondary-container` on the inactive ones. Between the ends, the handle sits over its stop, which the gap hides, so the stops between are placed 6px in from the track's ends, as Compose places them. A centered slider has none at its middle, which the gap beside the active track covers.
- **Value indicator:** the value in M3's Label Large weight (14px, 500), `inverse-on-surface` on an `inverse-surface` pill at least 48 by 44px, 12px above the handle.
- **Inset icon:** 24px on `md` and `lg` sliders and 32px on `xl`, 6px in from the start of its track (8px on `xl`): `on-primary` on the active track, `on-secondary-container` on the inactive one.

Disabled, the handles and active track are `on-surface` at 38% and the inactive tracks at 12%, with the stops `inverse-on-surface` on the active track and `on-surface` at 38% on the inactive ones.

The slider is at least 48px across its track, the handle's height when that's more, so it's always a large enough target. It mirrors in right-to-left layouts, starting from the right, and the left and right arrows follow it.

## Motion

A handle narrows and widens on the fast spatial spring, so it briefly overshoots, and colors, the focus ring and the value indicator change on fast effects (ADR-0018). The handle and tracks move to a new value at once, under the pointer, rather than animating. Under reduced motion, every state changes at once.
