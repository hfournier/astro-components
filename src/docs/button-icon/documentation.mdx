## Style by emphasis

Pick the style by how important the action is, from most to least emphasis:

- **Filled:** key, high-emphasis actions. Use it sparingly.
- **Tonal:** secondary actions next to a high-emphasis one.
- **Outlined:** medium-emphasis actions, such as moving through sets of cards.
- **Standard:** low-emphasis actions, or a button on a colorful surface.

When the button needs to stand out from its background, use filled, tonal or outlined rather than standard.

## Size

- Make the main action the most prominent, by color or size, as in play/pause or start/stop.
- Give buttons of similar importance the same size.
- Use only a few icon buttons at once. In dense layouts, group them in a toolbar or button group.

Don't shrink icon buttons to make a layout denser. Every size keeps a touch target of at least 48×48px (an invisible `::after` around the button), so don't override the `::after`'s size.

## Icons

M3's plain icon button uses a filled icon, such as `"star-fill"` rather than `"star"`.

## Toggles

Use a toggle for a binary action that the button turns on and off, such as favorite, bookmark or mute. Don't use one for an action with no selected state, such as opening an overflow menu.

A toggle uses the outlined icon while it's unselected and the filled one while it's selected. This isn't just style: M3 requires the selected state to show through at least two properties, not color alone. Give `icon` the outlined name, such as `"bookmark"`, and a toggle switches to `"bookmark-fill"` on its own. Almost every Material Symbol has a filled version; if `src/assets/svgs` doesn't have it yet, add it as `<name>-fill.svg` (and to `src/svgs/icon-names.ts`). For an icon whose filled and outlined shapes look the same, M3 makes the selected icon bolder (semibold or bold) instead, so supply a bolder SVG as `selectedIcon`.

A button that shows and hides other content, such as a menu or a panel, isn't a toggle: leave out `isToggle` and give it `aria-expanded` instead, as MenuToggle does. Never use both on one button.

## Opening a menu

A ButtonIcon opens a Menu, such as an overflow menu behind `more_vert`, with `command="toggle-popover"` and `commandfor` set to the menu's `id`. The menu gives it `aria-haspopup="menu"` and `aria-expanded`, and while the menu is open the button keeps its pressed state layer, as M3 asks.
