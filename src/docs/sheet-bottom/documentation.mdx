## Standard and modal

A standard sheet sits at the bottom of the main content, and both can be used at the same time. Put it last in a scrolling container (the page, or a scrolling region of it): it's sticky, so it stays at the container's bottom edge while the content scrolls behind it. Its drag handle collapses it to the handle and headline, and expands it again.

A modal sheet slides up over the page, and the page under its scrim can't be used until it closes. Use one for a set of choices, such as a share sheet or a menu on a small screen, that the user deals with before going back to the page.

## Layout

Following M3, the sheet spans the full width, up to 640px, centred, and in a window wider than 640px it also keeps 56px from the window's start and end edges. Its height follows its content, up to the window's height less a 72px top margin, or 56px in a window wider than 640px; past that, its content scrolls. It has extra-large (28px) top corners and sits at elevation 1. Its drag handle is 32 by 4px, centred, with 22px above and below it.
