---
title: Modal SheetBottom with scrolling content
description: A modal sheet opens at no more than half the window's height, and its content scrolls under the drag handle. When the content is taller than that, a click on the handle (or a drag up) expands the sheet to the window's height less M3's top margin, and another click returns it to half. As M3 asks of a sheet that reaches full height, its content starts with a row holding a heading, which names the sheet through `aria-labelledby`, and a close button with `command="request-close"`. The row is sticky, so it stays in place while the rest scrolls.
---
