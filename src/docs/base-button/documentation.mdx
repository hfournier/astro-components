## What the caller adds

BaseButton draws only the container. The component that composes it gives, through `class`:

- the corners and their morph (pressed, selected, or engaged as a split button's inner corners)
- the height, padding and layout
- the content and icons, and a toggle's `aria-pressed` with its selected shape

BaseButton leaves the corners out, so no caller has to override them. A caller's `class` is merged last, so it can also replace a color or the transition, as FabMenuItem does for its items.
