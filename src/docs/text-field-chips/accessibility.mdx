TextFieldChips keeps its input a native textbox named by the label, and puts it with the chips in a group named by the label too. Its label, supporting text, prefix, suffix, error state and character counter are TextField's, and named, described and announced as TextField's are: see [TextField's accessibility](/components/text-field).

Each chip is ChipInput's two buttons: its body, named by its label, and "Remove" and the label. Tab reaches each chip's body and remove button, then the input. In the text, Enter turns it into a chip; with the cursor at the start, Backspace selects the chip before it, focusing it, and ArrowLeft (ArrowRight right to left) moves focus to it. On the chips, the arrow keys move between them and, past the last, back to the text; typing goes on in the text. Shift with an arrow key moves the focused chip, and a polite status says where it went, such as "Blake, 2 of 2 in Cc", so moving doesn't depend on dragging. Removing the last chip puts focus back in the text.

On a page in another language, name the chips' remove buttons and edit fields in it: `removeLabel` and `editLabel` for the chips typed text becomes, and ChipInput's props of the same names for the `chips` slot's own.
