## Layout

Put the NavDrawer right after its NavRail (with `isExpandable={false}`), as siblings. From the large breakpoint up, the drawer takes its place in the layout, so give it a column, for example with a grid of `grid-cols-1 medium:grid-cols-[auto_1fr] large:grid-cols-[auto_auto_1fr]` on their parent. Below large it's a fixed overlay stacked under the rail, so it takes no space and slides out from under the rail's edge. Below medium the drawer isn't used: the rail is hidden until its MenuToggle opens it as a modal expanded rail (see NavRail), and the drawer moves its sections into the rail, after its destinations, where only the current page's section shows. From medium up they move back. It finds its rail and rail items by itself, matching each rail item's `href` to a section's `parentHref`, so there's nothing to wire up.

The drawer slides out from under the rail, so NavRail is `z-30`, the drawer `z-20` and its scrim `z-10`. Anything else on the page with a higher z-index shows above the open drawer.
