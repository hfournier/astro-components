## Props

MenuItem's props are MenuItemBase's, other than `role`, which it sets to `menuitem`, and the checkable items' `isChecked`, `checkedIcon`, `name` and `value`: `label` (required), `icon`, `trailingIcon`, `trailingText`, `supportingText`, `badgeLabel`, `href`, `isDisabled` and `submenu`. See MenuItemBase for each.

## Actions

Give the item its action as you would a button, with a `click` handler in a script, or with `command` and `commandfor` (to open a Dialog, say). Other button or link attributes, such as `id`, are passed through. Choosing it closes the menu once the click's handlers have run, and returns focus to the trigger, unless a handler has moved focus elsewhere.

## Appearance

The item is at least 44px high, with 8px above and below its content, 16px at each end, and 12px between its parts. It's 4px in from the menu's sides. Its label is `on-surface` (`on-tertiary-container` in a vibrant menu), and its icons, supporting and trailing text `on-surface-variant`. Its state layer is in its label's color, at 8% while hovered and 10% while focused or pressed. Its focus ring is drawn inside it, so a scrolling menu doesn't clip it.
