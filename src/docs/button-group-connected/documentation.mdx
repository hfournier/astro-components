## Attributes

Every attribute goes on the `<div>`. Name the group with `aria-label`, or `aria-labelledby` pointing at a visible heading, so assistive tech says what the choice is about.

## Anatomy

- **Container:** invisible. It spans the width it's in, its options sharing it equally, with 2px between them at every size. Each option is at least 48px wide. Add a class such as `max-w-120` to stop it growing too wide in a large window, or `w-fit` to hug its options.
- **Options:** M3's toggle buttons, each a `<label>` around its radio or checkbox. They take Button's height, padding, label type and icon size for their size, and an icon-only option an icon button's padding.

The group sets its options' corners:

| | Extra small | Small | Medium | Large | Extra large |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Inner corners | 8px | 8px | 8px | 16px | 20px |
| Pressed inner corners | 4px | 4px | 4px | 12px | 16px |

The first option's start corners and the last option's end corners are full on round options, and the inner corners' size on square ones. A selected option's corners are all full. These are the values in M3's tokens; the Specs page's prose gives extra small's inner corners as 4px.

## Motion

Only the option being pressed or selected changes shape. Its corners move on the fast spatial spring and its colors on fast effects, as a Button's do, and nothing around it moves. Under reduced motion, the corners and colors change at once.
