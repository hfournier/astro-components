## Attributes

Every attribute except `class` goes on the `<input>`: `name`, `value`, `checked`, `disabled`, `required`, `form`, `aria-*` and the rest. `class` goes on the item's `<label>`. The item takes ListItem's `overline`, `supportingText`, `icon`, `trailingIcon`, `trailingText` and `videoSize` too. Listen for the input's `change` event to react to a change.

## States

The checkbox is drawn at Compose's 24px, with no target or state layer of its own: the item is its target. The item shows M3's state layer, 8% hovered and 10% focused or pressed, and its corners morph from 4px to 12px hovered and 16px focused or pressed. A focused item shows a 3px `secondary` ring inside its edges.

Checked, M3 Expressive highlights the item: `secondary-container`, with its text and icons in `on-secondary-container` and 16px corners in every state. A disabled item's content is `on-surface` at 38%, and a disabled checked one is `on-surface` at 10%.
