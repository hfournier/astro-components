## Attributes

Every attribute, `class` too, goes on the item's `<div>`, `<a>` or `<button>`, such as `href`, `disabled` on a button, and `aria-*`. The `<li>` around it takes none.

## Anatomy

- **Container:** `surface`, with 4px corners, 16px of padding at each end and 10px above and below, and 12px between the leading element, the text and the trailing element.
- **Text:** an optional overline, the label, and optional supporting text, stacked.
- **Leading element:** one of an icon, an avatar, an image or a video. The media are decorative and hidden from assistive tech, since the label names the item.
- **Trailing element:** an icon or text.

## Heights and alignment

M3's heights follow the lines of text: 56px for the label alone, 72px with an overline or supporting text, and 88px with both, or with supporting text that wraps past one line. A taller leading element makes the item taller. The parts are middle-aligned, and top-aligned once the item has three lines or is 88px or taller, as M3's Specs ask. A script keeps the line count up to date as the item's width changes the wrapping.

## States

A static item has no states. A link or button item, on hover, focus and press, shows M3's `on-surface` state layer, 8% hovered and 10% focused or pressed, and morphs its corners, as M3 Expressive's shape morphing does: 12px hovered and 16px focused or pressed. Focused, it shows a 3px `secondary` focus ring inside its edges. A disabled button item shows its content in `on-surface` at 38%, and no state layer.
