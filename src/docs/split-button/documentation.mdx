## Attributes

`class` goes on the container, which lays the two buttons side by side. Every other attribute goes on the leading `<button>`, as it's a common button: `onclick`, `type="submit"`, `form`, `aria-label` and the rest. `disabled` disables both buttons.

## Anatomy

- **Leading button:** an icon, a label, or both, with Button's height, label type and icon size. Its outer corners are full and its inner corners small. Its padding is M3's split button spacing, a little tighter on the inner side in the two smallest sizes.
- **Trailing button:** the menu icon, `keyboard_arrow_down`, which M3 asks not to change, since it turns as the menu opens. It's as wide as it's tall from medium up, and 48px wide in extra small and small. Its icon sits slightly off center toward the leading button, so it looks centered beside the round end, until the menu opens.

The buttons are 2px apart in every size. Their inner corners widen while each is hovered, focused or pressed:

| | Extra small | Small | Medium | Large | Extra large |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Height | 32px | 40px | 56px | 96px | 136px |
| Inner corners | 4px | 4px | 4px | 8px | 12px |
| Hovered, focused or pressed | 8px | 12px | 12px | 20px | 20px |
| Menu icon | 22px | 22px | 26px | 38px | 50px |
| Menu icon off center | 1px | 1px | 2px | 3px | 6px |

While its menu is open, the trailing button is fully round and keeps a pressed state layer. Its colors don't change, unlike a toggle button's. The menu opens 4px below it, lined up with its start edge, or wherever there's room.

## Motion

The corners morph on the fast spatial spring and the colors change on fast effects, as a Button's do. The menu icon turns 180° inward as the menu opens and back as it closes, on the standard scheme's fast spatial spring, which M3 names for this turn. Under reduced motion, the corners and the icon change at once.
