## Behavior

MenuToggle is a disclosure button: it shows and hides other content, so its state is `aria-expanded` (announced as "collapsed" or "expanded"), not a toggle's `aria-pressed`. It flips `aria-expanded` on each click and swaps its icon to match. In an expandable NavRail, that's all it needs to do: the rail expands while it contains an expanded MenuToggle, purely in CSS. Below the lg breakpoint, every NavRail has one, fixed at the top left of the page while the rest of the rail is hidden, and the rail opens as a modal while it's expanded (see NavRail). Used on its own, it doesn't open anything: listen for its `click` event to show or hide whatever it controls, and give it `aria-controls` with that content's `id`. To start it expanded, pass `aria-expanded="true"`. Its label is "Menu", and like any ButtonIcon it shows a tooltip on hover and keyboard focus. Any button attribute you pass, such as `id` or `class`, goes to the button.
