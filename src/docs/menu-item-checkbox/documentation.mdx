## Choosing it

Choosing the item toggles `aria-checked` and fires a `change` event on its Menu, whose `detail` is `{ name, value, checked }`. The menu stays open, as M3's multi-select menus do, until it's dismissed.

## Appearance

A checked item has a `tertiary-container` container with `on-tertiary-container` content (`tertiary` and `on-tertiary` in a vibrant menu), 12px corners all round, and a check in place of its leading icon. It always keeps the leading icon's space, so the check doesn't move its label.
