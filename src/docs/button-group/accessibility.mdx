ButtonGroup is a `<div>` with `role="group"`, so assistive tech announces its buttons as one set. A single-select connected group is a `radiogroup` instead, and each of its buttons a radio, checked or not through `aria-checked`, as Compose gives its buttons the radio button role. A multi-select connected group's buttons stay toggle buttons. The group itself isn't focusable. Tab lands on its first button and moves through the rest, and the left and right arrow keys move between them too, mirrored right to left. The arrow keys stop at the ends and skip disabled buttons. Space or Enter presses the focused button, and in a connected group selects it. The arrow keys only move focus, so in a single-select group, unlike a native radio group, every button stays a Tab stop and moving to one doesn't select it, as M3's Accessibility page asks.

The group needs no name of its own, though `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby` can give it one. Label each button instead, as Button and ButtonIcon ask: an icon button's `label` is its name. A toggle button announces whether it's selected through `aria-pressed`. A selected button in a connected group doesn't rely on color alone either: it turns fully round, and a button with an icon shows it filled.

Each button keeps a 48px target. Extra small and small standard groups leave 18px and 12px between buttons so the targets don't overlap; don't reduce that space. A connected group's buttons are at least 48px wide, so their targets sit side by side.

Under reduced motion, pressing a button doesn't change any widths.
