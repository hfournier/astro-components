## Attributes

Every attribute goes on the `<div>`, which has `role="group"`, or `role="radiogroup"` for a single-select connected group. Name the group with `aria-label`, or `aria-labelledby` pointing at a visible heading, when a name helps say what its buttons are for. M3 doesn't require one: each button's own label is what matters.

## Anatomy

- **Container:** invisible, and as tall as its buttons.
  - **Standard:** it hugs its buttons. Between them it leaves M3's space for their size: 18px for extra small, 12px for small and 8px for medium, large and extra large. With mixed sizes it keeps the smallest one's space, so each button keeps its 48px target.
  - **Connected:** it spans the width it's in, its buttons sharing it equally, with 2px between them at every size. Each button is at least 48px wide. Add a class such as `max-w-120` to stop it growing too wide in a large window, or `w-fit` to hug its buttons.

The group reads the size from its buttons, so set `size` on each Button and ButtonIcon, not on the group.

In a connected group, the group sets its buttons' corners in place of their own:

| | Extra small | Small | Medium | Large | Extra large |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Inner corners | 8px | 8px | 8px | 16px | 20px |
| Pressed inner corners | 4px | 4px | 4px | 12px | 16px |

The first button's start corners and the last button's end corners are full on round buttons, and the inner corners' size on square ones. A selected button's corners are all full. These are the values in M3's tokens; the Specs page's prose gives extra small's inner corners as 4px. Use filled, tonal, outlined or elevated buttons; standard icon buttons and text buttons have no container to change width.

## Motion

Pressing a button, with a pointer or by holding Space or Enter, widens it by 15% of its width, and its neighbours give up the same width, so the group stays the same size. A middle button takes half from each side; an end button takes it all from its one neighbour. A neighbour gives up no more than the space beside its icon or label, so its content always stays inside it: for a small button, its 16px padding, and for a small icon button, the 8px either side of its icon. That limit is Compose's, which caps each neighbour at its trailing padding; M3's Specs page gives the 15% but no limit. The widths move on M3's fast spatial spring, and the group waits until the widening is three-quarters done before springing back, so a quick tap still shows it. Meanwhile the button's own corners morph as they do outside a group, and a toggle button swaps shape when it's selected.

A connected group widens nothing. Only the button being pressed or selected changes shape, its corners moving on the fast spatial spring and its colors on fast effects, as a Button's do.

Under reduced motion the widths don't change, and a connected group's corners change at once.
