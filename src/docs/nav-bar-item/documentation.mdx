## Compact and medium

Below the medium breakpoint (600px), the item is M3's vertical navigation bar item: the icon in a 56×32px active indicator, with the label in Label Medium 4px below it. It takes an equal share of the bar's width, and a label too long for it is cut off with an ellipsis. From medium up, it's M3's horizontal item: the icon and the label side by side 4px apart, in a 40px-high indicator that fits them with 16px either side.

## Current page

NavBarItem marks itself as the current destination (`aria-current="page"`) when its `href` is the current page's path or a parent of it, so `href="/components"` is also current on `/components/button`. `href="/"` is only current on the home page, or it would match every page. The current destination's indicator is in the secondary container color, with an `on-secondary-container` icon; its label is `secondary` below medium, where it's outside the indicator, and `on-secondary-container` from medium up, where it's inside it. To decide this yourself, pass `aria-current`.

## Badges

`badgeLabel` adds a Badge on the top-end corner of the icon, as in M3: a count, or a dot for `""`. Each item anchors its badge to its icon with its own anchor name, so there's nothing to set up. From medium up, where the label sits 4px from the icon, a count would cover the label, so it moves into the text flow after the label instead; a dot stays on the icon. Keep `badgeLabel` in step with the count it shows, since it also sets the item's accessible name: a count after the label ("Inbox, 3"), or `badgeDescription` for a dot ("Updates, New notification"). In a right-to-left page, the badge moves to the icon's top-left corner, its trailing corner there.
