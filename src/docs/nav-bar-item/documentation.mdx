## Compact and medium

Below the medium breakpoint (600px), the item is M3's vertical navigation bar item: the icon in a 56×32px active indicator, with the label in Label Medium 4px below it. It takes an equal share of the bar's width, and a label too long for it wraps onto another line, between words where it can. From medium up, it's M3's horizontal item: the icon and the label side by side 4px apart, in an indicator at least 40px high that fits them with 16px either side, centered in an item as wide as NavBar sets (see NavBar). A label too long for the item wraps inside the indicator, which grows taller.

## Current page

NavBarItem marks itself as the current destination (`aria-current="page"`) when its `href` is the current page's path or a parent of it, so `href="/components"` is also current on `/components/button`. `href="/"` is only current on the home page, or it would match every page. The current destination's indicator is in the secondary container color, with an `on-secondary-container` icon; its label is `secondary` below medium, where it's outside the indicator, and `on-secondary-container` from medium up, where it's inside it. With `activeIcon`, the current destination's icon also changes, to its filled version. To decide this yourself, pass `aria-current`.

Re-selecting the current destination scrolls the page back to the top instead of reloading it, when its `href` is the current page exactly (see NavBar's Accessibility).

## Badges

`badgeLabel` adds a Badge on the top-end corner of the icon, as in M3: a count, or a dot for `""`. Each item anchors its badge to its icon with its own anchor name, so there's nothing to set up. It stays on the icon at every width: from medium up, where the label sits 4px from the icon, a large count reaches over the gap and the start of the label, as in M3's own images. Keep `badgeLabel` in step with the count it shows, since it also sets the item's accessible name: a count after the label ("Inbox, 3"), or `badgeDescription` for a dot ("Updates, New notification"). In a right-to-left page, the badge moves to the icon's top-left corner, its trailing corner there.
