## Attributes

Every attribute except `class` goes on the `<progress>`: `value`, `max`, `aria-label`, `aria-labelledby`, `id` and the rest. `class` goes on the element around it, which draws the indicator, so it can place it.

- **Determinate:** set `value`, between 0 and `max` (1 by default). To show progress, set the progress's `value` from a script, as with any `<progress>`. The active indicator follows it.
- **Indeterminate:** leave `value` out. Setting one later turns the indicator determinate, as M3 suggests once a process's length becomes known. Removing the attribute turns it back.

## Anatomy

- **Active indicator:** `primary`, clockwise from the top of the ring to the value. Just started, it's a dot.
- **Track:** `secondary-container`, from a 4px gap after the active indicator round to a 4px gap before its start.

A circular indicator has no stop indicator. Center it on the element or page that's loading, or in the space where new content will appear. It isn't mirrored in right-to-left layouts: M3's circular indicators fill clockwise in every direction.

## Motion

A new value moves the active indicator and the gaps on the fast effects spring. ADR-0018 puts movement on fast spatial, but that spring overshoots, which would briefly show more progress than there is. The wave's amplitude, a shape, rises after 10% and flattens from 95% on fast spatial. The wave travels a wavelength a second, as Compose's does.

Indeterminate, the indicator runs Compose's 6 second cycle. The ring turns three times at a steady speed, plus a quarter turn in 300ms every 1.5 seconds. Meanwhile the active indicator grows from 10% to 87% of the way round, then shrinks back on standard easing. M3's Specs page gives no timing for it.

Under reduced motion, a new value and the wave's amplitude change at once, and the wave stands still. An indeterminate indicator holds still, its active indicator from 20% to 60% of the way round.
