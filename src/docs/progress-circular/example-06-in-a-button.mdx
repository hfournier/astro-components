---
title: In a button
description: Put the indicator in a button to show that its action is under way. Inside any of M3's buttons, as M3 asks, the active indicator takes the button's label or icon color, which contrasts with the button, and the track is removed. In small buttons, keep the flat shape, as the wave isn't as visible at that size.
