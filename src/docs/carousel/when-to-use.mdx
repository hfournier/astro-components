Use a carousel to browse a collection of visual items, such as photos, events or albums. The multi-browse layout shows several at once, of different sizes, and the previewed small item shows there's more to scroll to:

- **Visuals first:** each item is mainly an image or a video. Keep its text brief, two lines at most on a phone, and make sure the visual is still recognizable at the large size.
- **How many:** people expect around 10 items in a carousel like this. On a phone, show up to three items with text at once; more only when the visuals are simple.
- **One action per item:** each item opens what it shows, as a link, or does one thing, as a button.

### When not to use it

- **Lots of text, or other controls in the items:** use a list of Cards, or M3's uncontained carousel layout, which keeps every item at one size.
- **One large item at a time:** M3's hero layout, which spotlights one item, isn't part of Carousel yet.
- **Every item must be seen:** a carousel hides most of its items until it's scrolled. Use a grid or a list instead.
