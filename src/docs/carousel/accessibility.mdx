- **Role and name:** the carousel is a region, described as a carousel through `aria-roledescription`, as the WAI-ARIA carousel pattern has it, and named by its `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`; one without a name fails the build. Its items are a list, so screen readers announce how many there are and which one has focus, as M3's Accessibility page asks.
- **Keyboard:** the carousel itself isn't a tab stop, as M3 asks. Tab lands on its first item and moves through the rest, and the left and right arrow keys move between them too, mirrored right to left. The up and down arrow keys leave the carousel, to the control before or after it. Space or Enter activates a button item, and Enter a link item. Safari reaches links with Tab only when its "Press Tab to highlight each item" setting is on, or with Option-Tab.
- **Focus:** an item that takes focus while it's small scrolls into place, large, so it's seen in full. It shows a 3px focus ring 2px outside its edges.
- **Names:** each item is named by its content: a photo's `alt`, or the text in its `label` slot. When the text names the item, give the photo an empty `alt` so the name isn't read twice.
- **Show all:** on a page that scrolls vertically, add a Show all button or an arrow button beside the carousel's heading, leading to a page that lists every item, so people can reach them all without scrolling sideways (see Show all).
- **Pointer:** the row scrolls with a trackpad, a touch screen or Shift and a mouse wheel; it has no scrollbar, as M3 draws it, and no arrow buttons beside or over it, which M3 asks not to add.
- **Reduced motion:** there's no parallax, and the items don't change size: all are large, and the row scrolls to its edges.
- **Right to left:** the row starts at the right and scrolls to the left.
