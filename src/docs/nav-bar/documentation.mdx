## Compact and medium

The bar is at least 64px high, on `surface-container`. Below the medium breakpoint (600px), its destinations are M3's vertical items: each takes an equal share of the bar's width, with its icon in a 56×32px active indicator and its label 4px below it. From medium up, they're M3's horizontal items, grouped in the middle of the bar: each has its icon and label side by side in an indicator at least 40px high. The items follow the window's width rather than the bar's, since M3 bases the change on the window size class.

Horizontal items have a fixed width, and the extra space goes to the bar's ends, as in Compose: together the items take 60% of the bar for three destinations, 70% for four and 80% for five, each an equal share. An item whose icon and label need more room grows, up to an equal share of the whole bar, and the group stays centered. Each indicator hugs its icon and label in the middle of its item, and the whole item is the target.

M3 asks for labels of one or two words that fit on one line, neither wrapped nor truncated, so write them that way. Wrapping is there for larger text sizes: a label that no longer fits wraps onto another line, between words where it can, and the bar grows taller to hold it, so the full label stays visible. M3 only requires that up to twice the default text size, and allows truncation beyond it; this bar never truncates. A label that's too long wraps the same way, but that's a "Don't" in M3, not a way to fit a longer name.

## Layout

The bar is `fixed` to the bottom of the viewport at `z-30`. It adds the device's bottom safe area (`env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`) below its height, so on a phone with a home indicator it sits above the indicator, with its background filling the space under it. The safe area is only non-zero with `viewport-fit=cover` in the page's viewport meta tag. Since the bar is out of the page flow, leave room for it under the page's content, for example with `pb-[calc(4rem+env(safe-area-inset-bottom))]` on the `<body>`. That room only holds while every label fits on one line: a wrapped label, or larger text, makes the bar taller than 4rem. To leave room that grows with it, make the bar part of the page's layout instead: pass `class="sticky"` and put it last in a page that's at least the viewport's height, such as a `min-h-dvh` grid with `grid-rows-[1fr_auto]`, so it sticks to the bottom of the viewport and takes its own space.

M3 uses a navigation bar in compact windows and a navigation rail from medium up. For that pattern, put a NavBar and a NavRail on the same page, hide the bar from medium up with `class="medium:hidden"`, and hide the rail below medium with `class="max-medium:hidden"` (it then never opens as a modal). Both are labelled "Main", which is fine, since only one is shown at a time.

For a bar that isn't fixed, such as one inside a mockup, pass `class="static"`. The previews here do that, and give each bar its own `label`, so that they don't add more "Main" navigations to this page.
