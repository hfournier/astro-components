## Attributes

Every attribute, `class` too, goes on the item's `<a>` or `<button>`, such as `href`, `disabled` on a button, and `aria-*`. The `<li>` around it takes none.

## Anatomy

- **Item:** the visual, cropped to M3's extra-large corners (28px), with no container color of its own. Its width is set by the carousel: large, medium or small, depending on its place in the row.
- **Visual:** stays at the large size, centered on where the item is laid out, so the item's edges move over it as it changes size, M3's parallax.
- **Text (optional):** at the item's bottom start, light on a scrim that fades up from the bottom, so it reads over any visual.

## States

Hovered, focused and pressed, an item shows M3's state layer over its visual, `on-surface` at 8% hovered and 10% focused or pressed, and hovered it rises to elevation level 1. Focused, it shows a 3px focus ring 2px outside its edges. A disabled button item is at 38% opacity and can't be focused.
