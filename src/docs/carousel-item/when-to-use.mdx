Use carousel items for the entries of a Carousel. Each one is mainly visual:

- **Visual:** a photo or a video still that's easy to recognize at the large size, and still makes sense cropped to its middle when the item is small.
- **Text:** optional and brief, such as a title. It fades out as the item shrinks, so the item shouldn't depend on it being read at every size.
- **Action:** a link when the item opens what it shows, a button when it does something on the page.

### When not to use it

- **Several actions in an item:** an item does one thing. Use Cards for content with its own buttons.
- **Outside a Carousel:** an item takes its size from its carousel; on its own it has none.
