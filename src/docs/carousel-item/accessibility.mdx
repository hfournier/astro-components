Each CarouselItem is a `<li>`, so screen readers announce its position among the carousel's items. Its content is a link or a button:

- **Names:** an item is named by its content, its visual's `alt` and its `label` text. Give the visual an `alt` that says what the item opens or does, such as "Lake at sunrise", or an empty `alt` when the `label` text already names it.
- **Keyboard:** each item is a tab stop, and the carousel's arrow keys move between them. Enter opens a link item; Space or Enter activates a button item.
- **Focus:** a 3px focus ring 2px outside the item. A small item scrolls into place, large, when it takes focus.
- **Target size:** a large item is many times M3's 48px target. A small item is 40 to 56px wide, as tall as the carousel's items, and scrolls into place, large, when it takes focus.
- **Disabled:** a disabled button item can't be focused, and fades to 38%. The carousel's arrow keys skip it too.
- **Text contrast:** the `label` text is light on a dark scrim that fades up from the item's bottom edge, so it reads over a light visual too.
