Use filter chips to narrow a collection, such as search results or a list, by tags or descriptive words: "Docs", "Slides" and "Sheets" above a list of files, or "Pets allowed" above rentals. They're an alternative to checkboxes, or to a connected button group, when the filters sit right above the content they narrow.

- Name what to include, with a noun, and keep it short. Avoid negatives such as "Exclude images".
- Show chips in a set, never one alone, introduced by a label such as "Filter results".
- Keep every set on a page one kind: all multi-select, or all single-select.

### When not to use it

- **An action, such as "Add to calendar":** use ChipAssist.
- **A suggestion the product made, such as a reply:** use ChipSuggestion.
- **One choice that must always be made, in a form:** use ButtonGroupConnected's single select with `isSelectionRequired`, or radio buttons. A single-select ChipGroup suits one optional filter among a few.
- **A setting that applies at once, outside any list:** use a Switch.
