## Attributes

`class` goes on the element that holds the toolbar and its FAB, so classes that place it, such as `fixed bottom-4`, move them together. Every other attribute goes on the `<div>` with `role="toolbar"`. Name it with `aria-label`, or `aria-labelledby` pointing at a visible heading, after what its actions are for, such as "Photo actions". A toolbar without either fails the build.

## Placement

The page places a floating toolbar, as it does a docked one. M3 keeps it fully on screen, at least 16px from the window's edges, or 24px for a vertical one:

- **Horizontal, at the bottom:** `class="fixed bottom-4 left-1/2 z-1 -translate-x-1/2"`. To leave room for a phone's home indicator too, use `bottom-[max(--spacing(4),env(safe-area-inset-bottom))]`.
- **Vertical, at a side:** `class="fixed inset-e-6 top-1/2 z-1 -translate-y-1/2"`, on the opposite side from a [NavRail](/components/nav-rail).
- **Inside a scrolling box:** wrap it in `<div class="sticky bottom-4 flex justify-center">` as the box's last child.

It's only as wide as its items. When they don't fit, put the rest in a [Menu](/components/menu) opened from a More button at the trailing end, and show more of them as the window grows.

## Actions

Use icon buttons for an even set of actions, and emphasize at most one, so people find the action they use most:

- **An embedded primary action:** a filled or tonal ButtonIcon among standard ones.
- **A FAB beside it:** the page's highest-priority action, in the `fab` slot. Don't add a filled button as well.
- **A selected action:** a tonal toggle ButtonIcon (`variant="tonal" isToggle`) shows which of the toolbar's modes is on. In the toolbar, an unselected one has the toolbar's own colors and a selected one a secondary container (standard) or a surface container (vibrant), as M3's Specs give them.

Use round icon buttons, which match the toolbar's fully rounded shape, not square ones. In a vertical toolbar, use narrow or default icon buttons, not wide ones.

The toolbar is at elevation level 3, and a FAB beside it at level 1. Over a background that's distinct from it, M3 lets you remove the toolbar's elevation.

## Motion

With `scrollBehavior="exit-always"`, the toolbar and its FAB leave through the nearer edge of their scroll container, the window or a scrolling box: down or up for a horizontal toolbar, to the side for a vertical one. They move on M3's fast spatial spring, and with reduced motion they leave and come back at once.

With `scrollBehavior="collapse"`, the toolbar shrinks toward its FAB and the FAB grows in its place into M3's medium FAB: 80px, with 20px corners, a 28px icon and elevation level 2. Expanding reverses it. The toolbar's length and the FAB's size and corners move on fast spatial, its elevation on fast effects, and with reduced motion it changes at once. M3 asks that a toolbar not both collapse and leave the view, which one `scrollBehavior` can't do. Its buttons change color on M3's fast effects spring, and their corners morph on fast spatial, as they do anywhere.
