---
title: Exit on scroll
description: With `scrollBehavior="exit-always"`, the toolbar and its FAB leave as the content scrolls down, and come back as it scrolls up or when one of its controls takes focus. Here it's pinned with `sticky` at the bottom of a scrolling box; on a page, pin it to the window with `fixed`.
