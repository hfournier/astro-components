Use a card for content and actions about one subject, such as an album, a trip or a product, especially as an entry point to more detail. Cards are easy to scan in a grid, a list or a row, and each one puts its content in a clear order: media, a headline, supporting text, then actions.

The three styles work the same way and differ only in how much they stand out:

- **Filled:** subtle separation from the background, the least emphasis.
- **Elevated:** a shadow, more separation than a filled card.
- **Outlined:** a visible edge, the most emphasis.

Make a card interactive when the whole card leads somewhere or does one thing, such as opening an album. Leave it static when it only holds content and its own buttons and links.

### When not to use it

- **Content that spacing, headings or dividers can group:** don't force it into cards.
- **A single row of text in a list:** a [List](/components/list) is more compact.
- **Text or icons on an image:** avoid them, or add a scrim or a shape behind them so they keep their contrast.
