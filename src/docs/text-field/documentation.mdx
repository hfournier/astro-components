## Attributes

Every attribute except `class` goes on the `<input>`: `name`, `value`, `placeholder`, `required`, `disabled`, `readonly`, `pattern`, `maxlength`, `autocomplete`, `aria-*` and the rest. `class` goes on the element around the whole field, so it can place and size it. A field is 280px wide by default, Compose's minimum; set a width with `class`, such as `w-full`. Listen for the input's `input` or `change` event to react to a change.

A `placeholder` shows only while the field is focused and empty, since the resting label takes its place. Without a label, it always shows.

In MDX, leave a blank line between the label's text and each slotted element, or MDX puts them in one paragraph and the slots aren't found.

## States

- **Enabled:** filled, a `surface-container-highest` container with a 1px `on-surface-variant` indicator. Outlined, a 1px `outline` outline. The label is `on-surface-variant` and the input text `on-surface`.
- **Hovered:** the indicator or outline turns `on-surface`, and the filled container shows an `on-surface` state layer at 8%. The outlined label turns `on-surface`.
- **Focused:** the filled indicator thickens to 2px and the outline to 3px, both `primary`, as on M3's Specs page, and the label turns `primary`. The caret is `primary`.
- **Error** (`aria-invalid="true"`, or the browser's own validation once the person has interacted): the indicator or outline, label, caret, trailing icon and supporting text turn `error`, and hovered, the indicator, label and trailing icon turn `on-error-container`. See Accessibility.
- **Disabled** (`disabled`): everything in `on-surface` at 38%, the filled container at 4% and the outline at 12%.
- **Read-only** (`readonly`): styled as an enabled field, as M3 asks. Say it's read-only in its label or supporting text.

Both variants keep these roles on any background, as M3's Specs page maps them, so an outlined field inside a colored BaseWrapper still has an `on-surface-variant` label and supporting text and `on-surface` input text.

## Motion

M3's Specs page gives no timing, so TextField follows Compose: the label moves and resizes, the outline's gap opens, and the indicator or outline thickens on the fast spatial spring. Colors, the state layer, the prefix and the suffix fade on fast effects (ADR-0018). The placeholder fades in on slow effects as the field is focused, and out on fast effects. Under reduced motion, every state changes at once.
