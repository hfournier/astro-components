Use a text field to:

- let people enter or edit a short piece of text, such as a name, an email address or a number
- collect input in forms and dialogs
- search, with a leading search icon

Make text fields look interactive, and keep their labels and error messages brief and easy to act on. Use the filled or the outlined variant throughout a form, rather than mixing them without reason: filled fields stand out more, and outlined ones suit forms with many fields.

### When not to use it

- **Long answers:** a single-line field scrolls its text sideways. Use a multi-line field or text area.
- **Choosing from a fixed set of options:** use radio buttons, checkboxes or a menu.
- **Turning a setting on or off:** use a switch.
