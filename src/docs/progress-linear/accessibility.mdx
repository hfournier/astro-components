ProgressLinear is a native `<progress>`, so assistive tech announces it as a progress bar, with its value when it has one. An indeterminate indicator announces no value.

Name every indicator with `aria-label`, or `aria-labelledby` pointing at a visible heading. Say what's loading: the process and the content it affects, such as "Loading news article" or "Uploading photos", as M3 asks.

The indicator isn't focusable: it's a status, not a control. The active indicator and stop indicator are `primary`, at least 3:1 against the page's surfaces. Keep the stop indicator unless the track has 3:1 contrast with every container and surface around it, so the track's end stays easy to find.

A wavy indicator's wave and an indeterminate indicator's lines move continuously. Under reduced motion nothing moves: new values jump into place, the wave stands still, and an indeterminate indicator holds one line in the middle of its track. Name it well, since the name, not the movement, is then what says that work is going on.
