## Attributes

Every attribute except `class` goes on the `<progress>`: `aria-label`, `aria-labelledby`, `id` and the rest. `class` goes on the element around it, which draws the indicator, so it can place it. It takes no `value` or `max`: M3's loading indicator never turns into a determinate one. When a process's length can become known, use a circular or linear progress indicator instead.

## Anatomy

- **Active indicator:** `primary`, a shape 38/48 of the size that morphs through M3's SoftBurst, Cookie9Sided, Pentagon, Pill, Sunny, Cookie4Sided and Oval, then back to SoftBurst.
- **Container (optional):** a `primary-container` circle behind it; the active indicator turns `on-primary-container`.

Center it on the element or page that's loading, or in the space where new content will appear. It isn't mirrored in right-to-left layouts: like a circular progress indicator, it turns clockwise in every direction.

## Motion

It follows Compose's loading indicator, since M3's Specs page gives no timing. A new morph starts every 650ms, on Compose's spring for it (damping 0.6, stiffness 200), and turns the shape a quarter as it goes. The spring overshoots a little, and Compose stops it about 300ms in. Meanwhile the whole indicator turns once every 4666ms. That spring is Compose's own for the loading indicator, in place of ADR-0018's fast spatial, as ADR-0018 allows where the Specs page gives no motion.

The shapes and their morphs are a TypeScript port of AndroidX's graphics-shapes library, the one Compose draws them with, so each corner of one shape morphs into the nearest corner of the next. Copying the component means copying `src/shapes.ts` beside it too.

Under reduced motion it holds still on its first shape, SoftBurst, a quarter turn round.
