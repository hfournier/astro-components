As in M3, a badge belongs on a navigation destination, such as an item in a NavBar or NavRail, an app bar's action or a Tab, to show a notification, a count or a status:

- **A small dot** for an unread notification without a count.
- **A large badge** for a count or a short status, up to 4 characters.

When a badge marks unread notifications, hide it once its destination is selected, by no longer passing a `label` (or rendering the Badge) on that page.
