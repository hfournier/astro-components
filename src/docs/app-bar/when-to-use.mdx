Use an app bar at the top of a page for what relates to that page: a button to navigate, such as Back or a menu, the page's headline, and one or two key actions. It can also hold product-wide controls, such as search or notifications.

Pick the size for the headline:

- **Small** (`size="sm"`, the default) suits dense layouts, and pages that have been scrolled.
- **Medium flexible** (`size="md"`) shows a larger headline, which can wrap to a second line. It collapses into a small app bar on scroll.
- **Large flexible** (`size="lg"`) emphasizes the page's headline. It collapses into a small app bar on scroll too.

### When not to use it

- **More than two actions:** put them in a toolbar, not in an overflow menu in the app bar.
- **A long headline in a small app bar:** use a medium or large flexible app bar, which has room for two lines.
- **Moving between the app's top-level destinations:** use a [NavBar](/components/nav-bar) or [NavRail](/components/nav-rail). The app bar's leading button can open a navigation drawer.
