Use an app bar at the top of a page for what relates to that page: a button to navigate, such as Back or a menu, the page's headline, and one or two key actions. It can also hold product-wide controls, such as search or notifications.

The small app bar suits dense layouts, and pages that have been scrolled.

### When not to use it

- **Many actions:** put them in a toolbar.
- **A long headline, or one to emphasize:** use a medium or large flexible app bar, which has room for two lines.
- **Moving between the app's top-level destinations:** use a [NavBar](/components/nav-bar) or [NavRail](/components/nav-rail). The app bar's leading button can open a navigation drawer.
