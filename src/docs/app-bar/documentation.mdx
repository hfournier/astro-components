## Placement

Put the app bar in the page's `<Header>`, outside `<main>`, so it's in the page's banner landmark. Let it span the full width of the window, and keep its height and square corners.

To keep it at the top while the page scrolls, pin the header with `class="sticky top-0 z-1"`. The app bar fills with surface container while content is under it, so it stands apart from the content. An app bar that scrolls with its page has nothing under it, so it stays on surface. Inside a scrolling box instead of the page, pin the app bar itself with the same classes. It fills while that box is scrolled.

A pinned medium or large flexible app bar collapses into a small one as content scrolls under it. Its headline's row passes under the row of buttons, which stays where you pinned the bar, and the headline shows there in small. It stays small until scrolled back to the top. To do this, its script moves the pinned element up by the headline row's height, setting its `top`. Pin it with `sticky`: with `fixed`, it fills but doesn't collapse.

## Actions

M3 asks for one action, two if you need them. They should change or leave the whole page, such as Send, Save or Edit. If a page has more, put them in a toolbar. Avoid an overflow menu in the app bar where you can.

## Headline

The headline can name the current page, its section or the product. So it isn't a heading: the page's own `<h1>` stays in `<main>`. Keep the headline short enough to fit. In a small app bar, don't wrap it or make it larger. If it's long, or you want to emphasize it, use a medium (`size="md"`) or large (`size="lg"`) flexible app bar, which wraps it to a second line.
