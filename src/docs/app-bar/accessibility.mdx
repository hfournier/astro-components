- **Landmark:** in the page's `<Header>`, the app bar is in the banner landmark, so people can jump to it, and its actions stay reachable when it's pinned and the page has scrolled.
- **Headline:** it's text, not a heading, since it can name the product or a section rather than the page. Give the page its own `<h1>` in `<main>` and a `<title>` that says which page it is, so people know where they are.
- **Keyboard:** Tab moves through the leading button and then the actions, in order. Space or Enter activates the focused one. Each shows a focus ring.
- **Names:** name each icon button after what it does with its `label`, such as "Back" or "Search". The label is also its tooltip.
- **Targets:** each button has a 48px target.
- **Right to left:** the bar mirrors, the leading button at the right and the actions at the left.

Under reduced motion, it fills with color at once.
