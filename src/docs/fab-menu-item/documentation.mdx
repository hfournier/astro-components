## Actions

An item is always a `<button>`, never a link: a FAB menu holds actions. Give it its action as you would any button, with a `click` handler in a script, or with `command` and `commandfor` (to open a Dialog, say). Other button attributes, such as `id`, `value` and `disabled`, are passed through.

Choosing an item closes the menu once the item's own handlers have run, and returns focus to the FAB, unless the action has moved focus elsewhere, such as into a dialog it opened.

## Appearance

An item has Button's `lg` round size, which is M3's medium button, as M3 specifies (56px high, 24px padding at each end, 8px between its icon and its Title Medium label), at elevation 3, on the menu's container color. It has no `color` of its own: FabMenu's `color` sets every item's.

As M3 specifies, an item always has both its label and its icon, which tells the items apart at a glance, and hugs its content: don't give it a fixed width, truncate its label, or change its shape. Right to left, its icon follows its label.
