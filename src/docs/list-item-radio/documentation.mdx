## Attributes

Every attribute except `class` goes on the `<input>`: `name`, `value`, `checked`, `disabled`, `required`, `form`, `aria-*` and the rest. Give the items of one List the same `name`, so choosing one clears the others. `class` goes on the item's `<label>`. Its other props are BaseListItem's: `label` (required), `overline`, `supportingText`, `icon`, `trailingIcon`, `trailingText` and `videoSize`. See BaseListItem for each.

## States

The radio button is drawn at Compose's 24px, with no target or state layer of its own: the item is its target. The item shows M3's state layer, 8% hovered and 10% focused or pressed, and its corners morph from 4px to 12px hovered and 16px focused or pressed. A focused item shows a 3px `secondary` ring inside its edges.

Selected, M3 Expressive highlights the item: `secondary-container`, with its text and icons in `on-secondary-container` and 16px corners in every state. A disabled item's content is `on-surface` at 38%, and a disabled selected one is `on-surface` at 10%.
