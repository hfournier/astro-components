## Placement

A FabMenu isn't positioned on its own, as with Fab: put it where the FAB belongs, such as fixed to a corner of the page (e.g. `class="fixed right-4 bottom-4"`). The items open upward, 8px above the FAB, so leave room above it. It keeps the closed FAB's size while open, so nothing around it moves as the FAB turns into the close button.

The items are a popover, so they're drawn in the top layer: no ancestor's `overflow` or stacking order can clip or cover them. There's no scrim behind them, as in M3.

## Keyboard and screen readers

The FAB is a disclosure button: it's announced as expanded or collapsed, and its name stays `label` while it's open. The items follow it in the DOM, so Tab (or Down) from the open FAB moves to the top item. Closed, the items are hidden from everyone.

The menu closes, returning focus to the FAB, on Escape, a click outside it, the FAB again, or choosing an item (after the item's own handlers have run).

## Not in a NavRail

Don't put a FabMenu in a NavRail's `fab` slot. An expanded rail extends its FAB, and M3 doesn't open a FAB menu from an extended FAB (or from any other component).
