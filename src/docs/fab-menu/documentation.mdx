## Placement

A FabMenu isn't positioned on its own, as with Fab: put it where the FAB belongs. M3 places it in the end (trailing) corner of the window, 16px from its edges, increasing to 24px on large windows. For example, `class="fixed end-4 bottom-4 large:end-6 large:bottom-6"` (`end-*` keeps it in the trailing corner right to left). Keep it in the same corner at every window size, and use a larger FAB (`size`) on larger windows.

The items open upward from the FAB's top corner, 8px above it. The menu keeps the closed FAB's size while open, so nothing around it moves as the FAB turns into the close button.

The items are a popover, so they're drawn in the top layer: no ancestor's `overflow` or stacking order can clip or cover them. There's no scrim behind them, as in M3. Take care that they don't completely hide something interactive and its focus ring; covering part of it is fine.

## Short windows

When the window is too short for all the items, such as a phone in landscape, the list scrolls, as M3 specifies. It stays clear of the close button and 16px from the top of the window, with the top items showing first. It has no scrollbar: the cut-off item shows there's more, and moving focus to an item scrolls it into view.

## On the web

M3's guidelines say that on the web a FAB menu uses a menu component, and Google's Material Web library doesn't implement M3 Expressive. FabMenu brings the Expressive FAB menu to the web as Jetpack Compose builds it, at every window size.
