---
title: Persistent rich tooltip
description: Add `isPersistent` for a rich tooltip that a click on its element toggles, rather than hover or focus. It stays open when the pointer leaves, and closes on a click anywhere else, on Escape, or on a second click on its element. Its element must be a `<button>`, and shouldn't be an icon button.
