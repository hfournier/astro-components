## Placement

Use one FAB per screen, for its single most important action. A FAB isn't positioned on its own: it sits wherever you put it, such as in a NavRail's `fab` slot or fixed to a corner of the page (e.g. `class="fixed inset-e-4 bottom-4"`; `inset-e-*` keeps it in the trailing corner right to left). Use an extended FAB where a label makes the action clearer and there's room for it.

- **Compact and medium windows:** the lower trailing corner. An extended FAB can also sit at the bottom, centered.
- **Expanded and larger windows:** the upper leading region, such as a NavRail's `fab` slot. Screen reader and keyboard users also reach it earlier there. An extended FAB goes at the top of an expanded NavRail, or in the lower right corner, in both directions, rather than the trailing corner.

Leave 16px between a FAB and the window's edges. Don't put an extended FAB in the upper half of a phone screen, on top of toolbars, cards or other containers, or on the same screen as a floating toolbar.

A fixed FAB can partly cover the content it floats over, but it must never completely hide another control's focus indicator (WCAG 2.4.11). Leave room for it, such as with bottom padding on the page's content.

## Sizes

M3 recommends the medium FAB (`size="md"`) for most windows. Use the large FAB (`size="lg"`) where the action needs more prominence, or on expanded and larger windows. The default FAB suits compact windows with other actions on screen.

The same goes for an extended FAB: on a compact window with one prominent action, a large extended FAB can fit, and on larger windows, use a medium or large one.

## Collapse on scroll

M3 lets a page collapse an extended FAB into a plain FAB as the content scrolls, and extend it again when it scrolls back up or reaches the bottom. Fab doesn't watch the scroll itself, since that depends on the page: set and remove a `data-collapsed` attribute on the extended FAB from your own script. It animates between the two as M3's extended FAB does: the label fades out and the FAB shrinks to a square around its icon, with the tooltip back on while the label is hidden. A collapsed FAB needs an `icon`.

```js
const fab = document.querySelector("#compose");
let lastY = scrollY;
addEventListener("scroll", () => {
  const atBottom = innerHeight + scrollY >= document.body.scrollHeight - 1;
  fab.toggleAttribute("data-collapsed", scrollY > lastY && !atBottom);
  lastY = scrollY;
}, { passive: true });
```

## Don't disable it

A FAB has no disabled style. If its action isn't available, leave the FAB out rather than disabling it, as M3 asks.

## In a NavRail

In a NavRail's `fab` slot, the rail decides whether the FAB is extended, as M3 specifies: extended while the rail is expanded, and a plain FAB while it's collapsed, whatever `isExtended` says. It follows the rail's width, using a container query on the rail, so it animates between the two as the rail expands and collapses. Its tooltip follows too, showing only while the rail is collapsed and the label is hidden. As M3 asks of a FAB nested in another component, it sits at elevation 0 in the rail (and in a NavDrawer), at rest and on hover, rather than its usual 3 and 4.
