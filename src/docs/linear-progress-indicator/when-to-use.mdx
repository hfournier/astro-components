Use a linear progress indicator to show the status of a process that takes more than about 5 seconds, such as loading a page, submitting a form or saving changes:

- **determinate** when you know how far the process has got
- **indeterminate** when you don't, turning it determinate once you do

Place it along the edge of the container that's loading, or along the top of a page for the page as a whole.

### When not to use it

- **Waits under 200ms:** show the content straight away, without an indicator.
- **Waits from 200ms to 5 seconds:** use a loading indicator.
- **Several items loading together:** use one indicator for the whole group, not one per item.
- **An element narrower than 40px:** a linear indicator needs room. Use a circular one, centered on the element.
- **The same kind of process elsewhere uses a circular indicator:** use one kind of indicator for each process throughout a product.
