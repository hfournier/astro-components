---
title: DialogConfirm with Yes/No Buttons
description: A DialogConfirm whose actions are a plain Yes/No. Material 3 recommends naming the confirming action after what it does (e.g. "Delete"), but Yes/No can suit a direct question.
---

<Button command="show-modal" commandfor="dialog-yes-no">Open Confirm Dialog</Button>

<DialogConfirm
    id="dialog-yes-no"
    headline="Confirm Action"
    buttonDismiss={{ text: "No", value: "no" }}
    buttonConfirm={{ text: "Yes", value: "yes" }}
    >
    Are you sure?
</DialogConfirm>
