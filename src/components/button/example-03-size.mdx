---
title: Button size
description: You can change the button's overall scale with the `size` property. Text and padding scale together, so `sm` and `lg` are proportionally smaller/larger buttons, not just smaller/larger text. The `size` property works independently of `variant` and `color`.
---

    <Button size="lg">Large Button</Button>
</div>
