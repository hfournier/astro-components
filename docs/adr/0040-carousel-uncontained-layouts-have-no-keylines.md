# The uncontained carousels have no keylines, and the full-screen one scrolls vertically on Compose's

M3 Expressive's carousel has six layouts (#215 adds the last three). In the uncontained layout, items are "a single size and flow past the edge of the screen"; in the uncontained multi-aspect ratio layout, they're "of various sizes", "between 9:16 for min width size to 16:9 for max width"; the full-screen layout "shows one edge-to-edge large item", scrolls vertically, and must snap. ADR-0038 and ADR-0039 built the row, its items and the keylines behind the `layout` prop. We decided:

1. **`layout` also takes `"uncontained"`, `"uncontained-multi-aspect-ratio"` and `"full-screen"`.** As ADR-0039 has it, one component: the layouts share the row, the items, the keyboard and the build checks.
2. **The uncontained layouts have no keylines: their items keep their size, scroll natively and flow past the row's edges.** Compose's `uncontainedKeylineList` masks the item cut off at the end and the ones leaving at the start, so they shrink as they go. With M3's 16dp leading padding, its start steps also shrink the first items at rest (186dp items measured 178px until the row scrolled). M3's Specs ask for items of one size that "bleed over the padding on each side when scrolling", so we follow them, and there's no parallax in these layouts. An uncontained carousel's items are `preferredItemWidth` wide, 186px by default, as Compose's sample. Both layouts scroll freely (M3's Guidelines: default scrolling is "recommended for uncontained layouts"), and have leading padding only, as M3's Specs measure them. A focused item that's partly cut off scrolls into view, the least way, as the browser doesn't move one already partly showing.
3. **An uncontained multi-aspect ratio carousel's items take their width from CarouselItem's `aspectRatio`, such as `"16:9"`, at the row's height.** M3's Specs name the items by their ratio, so the prop is M3's; a ratio outside 9:16 to 16:9 fails the build (ADR-0021). Compose has no such layout, and its keylines are for items of one size. The row sets the width from the ratio only in this layout, so other layouts ignore it; an item without one is `preferredItemWidth` wide.
4. **The full-screen layout scrolls vertically, one item at a time, on Compose's uncontained keylines for an item the carousel's size.** That's one large item between two anchors, which Compose's `UncontainedTest` calls "a fullscreen layout", and MDC's `FullScreenCarouselStrategy` draws the same; the item leaving shrinks over its visual, the parallax. `src/carousel.ts` ports `uncontainedKeylineList` exactly, checked against `UncontainedTest`. The row has no padding and 16dp between items, as M3's Specs measure, and every item is a snap stop the row can't scroll past (`scroll-snap-stop: always`, as ADR-0039). Up and down move between its items, through `moveFocusWithArrows`' new `isVertical` option, since a region can't take `aria-orientation`. Across it, left and right leave the carousel, mirrored right to left, as up and down leave a horizontal one. The carousel is the window's height (`h-dvh`) by default, as M3's Specs ("fill the window edge-to-edge"); a `preferredItemWidth` fails the build. Its items keep M3's 28dp corners, as MDC recommends `shapeAppearanceExtraLarge` for them; M3's full-screen measurements list no corner radius, unlike every other layout's, so this is MDC's call where the Specs are silent.

We rejected keylines for multi-aspect ratio items built on Compose's, which would size every item alike. We rejected a horizontal full-screen layout: M3's Guidelines have it scroll vertically, and MDC recommends a vertical carousel for it.

## Consequences

- The uncontained layouts have no parallax, where Compose's uncontained carousel has it at the edges. The Overview's "item visuals have a parallax effect when scrolled" holds for the other four.
- With reduced motion, the uncontained layouts look as they do without it, and a full-screen item doesn't shrink as it scrolls out.
- A full-screen carousel needs no Show all button (M3's Accessibility page).
- In an uncontained multi-aspect ratio carousel, an item without `aspectRatio` is `preferredItemWidth` wide rather than failing the build: a CarouselItem renders without knowing its carousel's layout.
- CarouselItem's visual and text now take the large size from `--ac-carousel-visual-size`, which the script sets with the keylines, and fill the item without them, so an item sized by its aspect ratio crops its visual to its own width.
